Good to know

Questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Email us and we’ll get back to you.

Is there a minimum guest headcount for catering events?

We do not have a minimum headcount requirement. For small events and parties we may suggest opting for the basic package. Happy to discuss options!

What areas of Southern California do you serve?

We are currently scooping in the greater Los Angeles area, Orange County and the Inland Empire. For parties outside those areas, just drop us a note!

Do you offer flavors that are not listed on your website?

For additional or custom ice cream flavors, please contact us and we will do our best to accommodate your request!

Do you offer toppings that are not listed?

As a family owned and operated business, we strive to accommodate everyone’s favorite toppings! Please contact us to discuss how we can make your topping dreams come true.

Do you offer discounts to churches or non-profits?

We love working with local churches and non-profits! Please reach out to discuss further.

Do you ship or wholesale ice cream?

At this time we do not offer shipping, and we are not wholesaling our ice cream. Please check back with us in the future!

Can I buy into or franchise the Pink Lady brand?

As a family owned and operated business, we take pride in our offerings and services, and feel this is best achieved by remaining a family owned company.
