About Trish

Trish

Patricia “Trish” Steele

Patricia “Trish” Steele is a humanitarian, transformational speaker, author, mentor, and founder of Safe Passage Heals, a nonprofit organization dedicated to helping women and children affected by domestic violence rebuild their lives with dignity, confidence, and independence.

Drawing from her own journey through adversity and transformation, Trish has spent decades turning personal challenges into a life of service. Her work centers on resilience, purposeful living, longevity, wellness, beauty from the inside out, faith, women’s empowerment, and the belief that our past does not have to determine our future.

An accomplished author and experienced media personality, Trish has used television, speaking, writing, mentoring, and humanitarian advocacy to inspire others to rediscover their strength and create meaningful new chapters in their lives.

At the heart of Trish’s work is a message that has shaped both her personal journey and her service to others:

“
Your story is not finished. It is never too late to heal, love, rediscover your purpose, and create an extraordinary new chapter.
Trish Steele
